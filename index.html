Game log

    One Moon is flipped at the start of each round. Its rule lasts that round unless it says otherwise.

    One Hunt hides in each set of the Moon deck. When it comes up, every player's Health changes (usually -1, rarely 0 or +1), then its effect hits everyone. The last one ends the game.

    Each round

    1. Flip the top card of the Moon deck. A Moon changes the rules for this round. A Hunt hits every player right away; if it was the last Hunt, the game ends.
    2. Turns go clockwise: play 1 trait from your hand onto your monster and do its effect.
    3. Regen: at the end of your turn, draw or discard until the cards in your hand equal your Health (never below 2). You pick which cards to discard.

    Scoring after the last Hunt

    • Points on your traits, plus tokens and end-of-game effects.
    • Top of the family: most traits of a family = +3 (ties: +1 each).
    • Full set: traits from all 6 families = +4.
    • Most points wins. Tie: fewer traits wins.
