Round 1 is played before dark. After every round a Night card is flipped; its rule lasts through the next round unless it says otherwise.

The villagers are coming for your monster army: one Hunt hides in each set of the Night deck. When it comes up, every player's Health changes (usually -1, rarely 0 or +1), then its effect hits everyone. The last one ends the game.

Gear attaches to a trait: play it (that's your play for the turn) and drag it onto one of your traits. A trait can wear any number of Gear and adds their points. If the trait is removed, its Gear is discarded; if an opponent steals or swaps it, the Gear goes with it. Cursed Gear goes on an opponent's trait. Many traits fight over Gear: steal it (some only from one family), wreck it, dig it out of the graveyard, push Cursed Gear onto enemies or shake it off, protect it, or score for hoarding it.

Defense cards are played face down (that's your play for the turn). You can have 2 face down at a time. Opponents only see how many you have. Each one flips up by itself the moment what it names happens, then goes to the graveyard.

Each round

  1. Night falls (not before round 1): flip the top card of the Night deck. A Night changes the rules for this round. A Hunt (the villagers) hits every player right away; if it was the last Hunt, the game ends.
  2. Turns go clockwise: play 1 trait from your hand onto your monster and do its effect.
  3. Regen: at the end of your turn, draw or discard until the cards in your hand equal your Health (never below 2 or above 10). You pick which cards to discard.

Scoring after the last Hunt

  • Points on your traits, plus their Gear and end-of-game effects.
  • Top of the family: most traits of a family = +3 (ties: +1 each).
  • Full set: traits from all 6 families = +4.
  • Most points wins. Tie: fewer traits wins.
